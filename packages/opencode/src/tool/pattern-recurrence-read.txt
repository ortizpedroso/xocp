Reads the project-wide recurrence report: criteria/rules that failed (review_checklist or baseline-auditor) across ≥3 distinct completed tasks within the last 20 completed tasks or 30 days (whichever window is smaller), plus self-test escalations from Tarefa 8. No task_id needed — this looks at the whole project, not one task.

Separates two categories: "erro recorrente" (a criterion/rule repeatedly failing — candidate for a new baseline rule or a prompt fix) and "rotina recorrente" (the same manual work routine repeating across ≥5 distinct tasks without any rejection, as labeled by the workflow-executor via record_rotina_event — candidate for a new tool/skill). The pattern-auditor only reports candidates; it never implements them.
